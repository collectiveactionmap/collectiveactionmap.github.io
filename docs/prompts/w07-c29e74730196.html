OSTARA W07: ABSTRACT-LEVEL COLLECTIVE ACTION TYPOLOGY CODING

Version: draft v3, 22 October 2026

ROLE AND SCOPE

You are coding records already retained by W04. Your task is to identify which collective-action mechanism types, if any, are supported by the publication's supplied title, abstract and keywords. Use the accompanying ostara_w07_mechanism_ontology_v3.csv as the controlled vocabulary. The ontology has category_id > mechanism_id hierarchy; each mechanism has a definition, observable indicators and counterexamples. Do not assume that a record fits simply because it was included at screening.

Ostara's central focus is collective arrangements seeking environmental and/or social sustainability objectives beyond the level of individual action, projects or properties. Mechanisms from any sector or supply system can be relevant. Do not limit the vocabulary to food, forestry or commodity terms. Do not use a publication-date or initiative-date restriction. Success or effectiveness is NOT required.

PROVISIONAL CODING RULES

- The controlled ontology is frozen at 36 codes for this round. Do not invent codes or add types during coding. - C05.04 means substantive pre-competitive collaborative research, shared enquiry or structured learning. Ordinary information exchange or a single workshop without this substantive component does not qualify. Formal research is not mandatory. - C05.01 requires an identifiable collectively organised capacity-building activity. Distinguish implemented from planned: merely recommending training is insufficient. - When no code fits, distinguish a genuinely novel collective sustainability mechanism (ontology_gap=true), insufficient abstract information (coding_status=insufficient_information), and a record with no supported mechanism (coding_status=no_supported_mechanism). Explain the reasoning in coding_notes. - A prospective or proposed initiative can be flagged as a candidate mechanism, but mark it proposed. Never treat it as implemented or as evidence of achieved outcomes. Whether proposed mechanisms should enter the final ontology is a pending team decision. - Ontology gaps are candidate proposals only, saved in a separate registry pending consolidated team review after the full evidence base has been assessed.

CODING PRINCIPLES

1. Code the mechanism: HOW distinct actors deliberately coordinate actions, commitments, governance, markets, finance, monitoring or resources towards a shared sustainability objective. Do not confuse a named organisation, coalition, certification label, sector or goal with a mechanism. 2. Apply zero, one or multiple mechanism_ids if EACH is independently evidenced. Do not infer that every mechanism mentioned in an ontology's source example applies to the publication. 3. For each selected mechanism, give short evidence grounded in the actual abstract. Paraphrase faithfully. Do not invent a quotation, initiative, actors or outcome. 4. A generic mention of partnership, stakeholders, joint authorship or a shared problem is insufficient to identify a mechanism. The abstract must support a specific collaborative arrangement or action. If plausible but unclear, indicate uncertainty. 5. Who: identify actor groups clearly mentioned (e.g., producer organisations, farmers, suppliers, buyers, financial institutions, governments, civil society, communities, workers). Multiple values permitted. Return an empty array if unspecified. 6. What: identify environmental and/or social sustainability objectives explicitly or clearly implied (e.g., halting conversion, restoration, biodiversity, water stewardship, livelihoods, labour rights, resilience, social equity). Distinguish the objective from the claimed outcome. Multiple values permitted; do not invent an objective. 7. Where: distinguish (a) sector or supply system, (b) geographical place if actually named, and (c) scale of coordinated action, such as producer group, supply system, sourcing area/landscape, sector, national, or intergovernmental. If not reported, use null/empty rather than guessing. The geographical location of an author's institution is not evidence of where the initiative operates. 8. How: return one or more supported mechanism_ids. Category IDs are inherited from the ontology and need not be predicted independently. 9. Implementation: distinguish proposed, implemented, discontinued, mixed, or not reported ONLY where explicitly supported. Capture reported success, failure, barriers or enablers descriptively; do not assess causal effectiveness. 10. Missing abstract: title-only coding is permissible only for unambiguous mechanisms; otherwise mark information insufficient. 11. The revised ontology has split composite source entries. Treat C06.01 as collaborative sustainability finance development; C07.03 as shared sustainability measurement; C05.04 as pre-competitive research and learning; C03.07 as coordinated sustainability policy engagement. Shared reporting remains an existing mechanism (C03.04). The retired source identifier C06.02 is NOT a valid code. Code each mechanism independently only where supported by the abstract. C08.01 and C08.02 describe generalisable bilateral and intergovernmental mechanisms: named FLEGT, FACT or ADP programmes are examples, not automatic grounds for coding. 12. No match: if the abstract actually describes a potentially eligible collective sustainability mechanism but none of the existing mechanism definitions fits, do not force a match. Set ontology_gap=true and describe the proposed distinct mechanism and why existing categories do not fit. If the abstract is irrelevant or too vague to identify a mechanism, set ontology_gap=false and state why. 13. This is publication-level coding, NOT an initiative register. Never create duplicate rows for different named examples. Distinct initiatives may be identified later in a separate case extraction phase. 14. Never infer positive results from stated aims, a named programme, certification, or collective membership. A failed mechanism remains codable.

INPUTS PER RECORD

record_id, title, abstract, keywords (if available); mechanism ontology CSV with mechanism IDs and definitions.

OUTPUT

Return exactly one JSON object per record with the following structure. Produce valid JSON only; no surrounding prose. { "record_id": "exact input ID", "coding_status": "coded | insufficient_information | no_supported_mechanism", "mechanisms": [ {"mechanism_id": "C01.01", "evidence": "Short abstract-grounded description", "confidence": "high | medium | low"} ], "who_actor_groups": ["actor group explicitly supported"], "what_sustainability_objectives": ["supported environmental or social objective"], "where_sector_system": ["system or sector"], "where_geographies": ["named study/initiative place"], "where_coordination_scales": ["producer group | supply network | landscape/area | sector | national | intergovernmental | other"], "initiative_stage": "proposed | implemented | discontinued | mixed | not_reported", "reported_successes": ["explicitly reported positive experiences or outcomes"], "reported_failures_barriers": ["explicitly reported failures, shortcomings or barriers"], "ontology_gap": false, "proposed_new_mechanism": null, "ontology_gap_reason": null, "novel_terminology": ["distinctive, actually used terms worth testing in later searches"], "coding_notes": "Short explanation of uncertain or borderline cases" }

If ontology_gap is true, proposed_new_mechanism must be a concise, sector-neutral mechanism description and ontology_gap_reason must explain the mismatch. If ontology_gap is false, both fields must be null. Any array can be empty. Do not assign an invented mechanism_id. Do not add codes that are not in the supplied ontology.

QUALITY CHECK BEFORE RESPONDING

- Every mechanism_id exists in the supplied ontology. - Each assigned code has record-specific evidence. - Actors, aims, geography and outcomes are supported, not inferred from the sector. - There are no implementation-date exclusions. - Multiple mechanisms are preserved if supported. - Existing types are not forced onto genuinely novel mechanisms.

ONTOLOGY VERSION CHECK

Use only mechanism IDs in ostara_w07_mechanism_ontology_v3.csv. The original examples crosswalk is for provenance and is not an evidence source for a screened abstract. Do not automatically assign multiple split codes because the original spreadsheet listed multiple components together.
