CoalitionThe coalition that gave rise to and implements the collective action case, applicable only when there is a formally established coalition. This is not a comma-separated list of the actors coming together to deliver the collective action initiative, therefore if there is no formal coalition identified to initiate and implement this case, leave this field blank.Free textFALSE
Who are the contributing actors?A comma-separated list of actors who contribute resources to the collective action initiative. Includes actors that contribute to and/or implement the initiative.Free textTRUE
Who are the core implementing actors?If evident, the subset of the actors who implement, convene, or orchestrate the collective action.Free textTRUE

Case descriptionA description of what the collective action case does.Free textTRUE
What is the level at which the collective action operates? One of the following: -private sector, -place- or landscape-based, -national, -inter-governmental or multinational, -systemic or otherFixed, one optionTRUE
What are the objectives/intentions of the collective action?Summary of what the collective action actors state as the objectives of the initiative and what it intends to achieve.Free textTRUE
What is/are the shared interest(s) that motivate actors to initiate, contribute to and/or implement the initiative as a collective?One or more of the following: shared risks, shared costs, shared benefits, shared value creation. Definitions for each: -shared risk= actors are exposed to adverse outcome(s) anticipated with relative confidence, which is/are not avoidable by unilateral action -shared costs= actors incur expenditure(s) separately to satisfy the same requirement or generate the same information; -shared benefits= actors gain resources, capabilities and/or datasets whose value is shared, with shared governance structures for it/them; -shared value creation= actors create value not presently existing which is contingent on joint action to bring into being (e.g. new markets, propositions or premia unavailable to actors individually)Fixed, multiple options possibleTRUE
How are progress and success measured by actors?Evidence for how actors (those that initiate, contribute to and/or implement the initiative) measure the progress and success of the initiative. This can include how they measure the impact on the ground, but is not always the case. Some initiatives will not measure progress or success, in which case use "N/A".Free textFALSE
To what extent was/is the initiative successful in achieving its stated objectives, according to the actors? Summary of the evidence of positive progress / success of the initiative as measured by the initiative actors, if describedFree textFALSE
To what extent did/does the initiative fail to achieve its stated objectives, according to the initiative architects?Summary of the evidence of negative progress/ failure of the initiative as measured by the initiative actors, if describedFree textFALSE
To what extent did/does the initiative fail to achieve its stated objectives, according to any independent assessment? Summary of most up to date evidence of negative progress/failure of the initiative as credited by independent assessors, if describedFree textFALSE
How is impact measured?Evidence for how the impact on the ground from the initiative is measured. This can be a part of how initiative actors and/or independent assessors measure progress and/or success but is not always the case.Free textFALSE
What were the positive environmental and social impacts of the initiative? Summary of evidence on the environmental and social impacts on the ground as measured and/or credited by initiative actors and/or independent assessorsFree textFALSE
In what ways did the initiative strengthen the enabling conditions for collective action? Summary of evidence on how the focal initiative made collective action initiatives in general more likely to form and/or succeed by strengthening the enabling conditions for this.Free textFALSE
